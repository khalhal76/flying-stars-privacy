Flying Stars Feng Shui — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026 · App: Flying Stars Feng Shui (com.simplefoods.flyingstars) · Android and iOS

Flying Stars Feng Shui helps feng shui consultants prepare a flying star study of a home: the floor plan, the people who live there, and advice room by room. This page explains exactly what leaves your device and what does not.

There is no account

The app has no sign-up, no login and no user profile on any server. We do not know who you are, we cannot see your projects, and we have no way to contact you.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into a project stays only on this device:

  • floor plans and room photos or PDFs you add, and any area cut out of them
  • the property's name, build or renovation date and entrance direction
  • the names, dates of birth, gender and household roles of the residents you add, which are used on the device to work out each person's number and best directions
  • furniture you place, and any wording you change in the report
  • the report pages you save, which go wherever you choose to save or share them

None of this is uploaded, synced or visible to us. The flying star chart, the residents' numbers and all the advice are worked out on the device itself. Deleting a project deletes it from the device; deleting the app deletes every project, with no copy anywhere else.

About the people in a study

If you are a consultant, the residents you add are usually your clients and their family. Their details never leave your device through this app, but you remain responsible for having their permission to record them and for how you share the reports you create.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat on top of the App Store or Google Play. Payment is taken by Apple or Google — we never see your card details. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app and the purchase receipt supplied by the store, so the app can tell whether your subscription is active. It receives nothing from your projects.

We do not sell data. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in this app.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, so there is nothing held by us to delete on request.

Your control

  • Delete a project — from the project library, removed from the device immediately.
  • Uninstall the app — erases every project, picture and setting.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription — in the App Store or Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device — for example if accounts or syncing between devices are added — the effective date above will change and the updated policy will be published here before the change reaches the app.
