App: Flying Stars Feng Shui (com.simplefoods.flyingstars) · iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

Flying Stars Feng Shui turns a floor plan into a flying star study: the chart laid over the plan, notes for every area of the home, a page about the people who live there, and advice room by room — where the bed, desk, cooker and table go.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with your email address — we send you a one-time code, so there is no password to remember — or with Sign in with Apple on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Signing in for the first time makes your account.

I didn't get the sign-in code.

It can take a minute to arrive. Check your junk or spam folder, make sure the email address is typed correctly, then ask for a new code. Only the newest code works.

Can I use my projects on more than one device?

Yes. Projects are saved to your account as you work. Sign in with the same account on another iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android device and they are there. Each device also keeps a copy, so the app works while you are offline and catches up when the connection is back.

What does the free version include?

The main floor plan, one room page, one resident, and one report. Once that report has been saved the project is kept as it is: you can download it again at any time, but not change it.

What does a subscription add?

Everything, without limits: every resident, every room, furniture on the plan, rewording the report in your own words, as many reports as you need, and a library of projects. A subscription belongs to your account, so it works on every device you sign in to.

How is the chart worked out?

From the date the home was built or last renovated, which gives its period, and the exact direction its entrance faces, to a tenth of a degree. The direction is always given precisely — for example North 1 — because two homes facing "north" can have different charts.

The grid does not line up with my floor plan.

The app proposes a position from the walls it finds. Drag the grid to move it, drag a corner to resize it, and use the round handle to turn it so north points the right way.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On a Mac: App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; your projects stay in your account.

Deleting your data

Delete a project from the project library: it is removed from your account and from all your devices.

Delete your account inside the app: open the project sheet, and under your email address choose delete account. Your account, every project and every picture are deleted from our servers straight away. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address you sign in with and we will delete it for you.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, which is held by Apple or Google — cancel it there as shown above.
